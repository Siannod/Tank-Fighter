<h1>Tank Fighter</h1>
I made tank fighter as an initial project using Unreal Engine 5.6; the development of the game was focused more on creating gameplay mechanics rather than refining and optimising those mechanics
<h2>Controls</h2>
WASD - Movement<br>
1 2 3 4 - Select weapon<br>
LMB - Fire Weapon<br>
SPACE - Dash<br>
R - Reload<br>
<h2>Weapons</h2>
There are four weapons in the game:
- Base Weapon <br>
- Fast Weapon <br>
- Grenade Launcher <br>
- Shotgun <br>
This section will go over each weapon in detail.
<h3>Base Weapon</h3>
Magazine Size: 10<br>
Fire Rate: 0.3sec<br>
Reload Time: 2 sec<br>
DPS: 33.3<br>
Description: The base weapon is an automatic weapon, given to the player on spawn.
<h3>Fast Weapon</h3>
Magazine Size: 20<br>
Fire Rate: 0.2sec<br>
Reload Time: 1 sec<br>
DPS: 25<br>
Description: The fast weapon is another automatic weapon, the second gun given to the player.
<h3>Grenade Launcher</h3>
Magazine Size: 3<br>
Fire Rate: 1 sec<br>
Reload Time: 5 sec<br>
DPS: 10<br>
Description: The Grenade Launcher functions very differently from the other weapons, the longer you hold down the button the higher the gun will aim, upon release the projectile will fire causing damage within a radiues of landing, the gun can also be used to destroy stones you may find in your way.
<h3>Shotgun</h3>
Magazine Size: 2<br>
Fire Rate: 0.5 sec<br>
Reload Time: 1 sec<br>
DPS: 100 <br>
Description: The shotgun weapon fires out a shell of 10 in a cone in the direction of fire allowing the player to hit multiple enemies in one shot or deal mass amounts of damage to an enemy close up.
